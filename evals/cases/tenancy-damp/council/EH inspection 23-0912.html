Summary of findings

The inspection followed a complaint from the tenant received on 22 May 2023. Extensive black mould growth was observed to the rear bedroom external wall (approx. 2.5 m²) and to the window reveal. Moisture meter readings on the external wall were in the wet range throughout. There is no mechanical extract ventilation in the bathroom or kitchen. The window sill is decayed.

HazardBandCategoryNotes
Damp and mould growthC1Child occupant with asthma - vulnerable group
Excess coldE2Single glazing to rear bedroom, decayed frame

A Category 1 hazard places a duty on the Council to take the most appropriate enforcement action. In view of the previous lack of response to the tenant's complaints, the Council intends to serve an Improvement Notice under section 11 of the Housing Act 2004 on the owner.

Action taken

Improvement Notice ref. IN/23/0912 was served on Mr G. Whitfield by first class post on 19 June 2023, requiring works to remedy the damp and mould hazard (including a damp-proof treatment to the external wall, mechanical extract ventilation to the kitchen and bathroom, and replacement of the rear bedroom window) to start no later than 17 July 2023 and be completed within 8 weeks of that date.
