I am the GP for Leo Kim and his mother, Ms Sarah Kim.

Leo has had asthma since the age of four, which was well controlled until the
end of last year. Since November 2022 his symptoms have become markedly worse.
He was taken to the Emergency Department at Bristol Royal Hospital for Children
on 2 February 2023 with an acute asthma attack and needed nebulisers and oral
steroids, and he has needed two further courses of steroids since.

His mother tells me there is extensive black mould in his bedroom. Exposure to
damp and mould is a well-recognised trigger for asthma in children, and in my
opinion the condition of the bedroom is very likely to be contributing to the
deterioration in Leo's health.

I would strongly support urgent action to remove the damp and mould, or
alternative accommodation for the family if that is not possible.
