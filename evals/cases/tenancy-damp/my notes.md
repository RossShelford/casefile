# Flat 3 - damp diary (Sarah)

Keeping this because the CAB adviser said to write everything down.

## 2023

**28 March** - rang Tom again, voicemail. Again. Left message re: GP letter, said I'd email it.

**Weds 29 March 2023** - emailed Tom the GP letter. No reply.

**22 May** - Gave up on Harbour. Filled in the council's online form for a housing complaint (ref 23/0912).

**6 June** - Council lady (Leanne Moss) came. Took ages, meter on walls, photos. Tom turned up too, very quiet. She said it's "Category 1" and she will be serving a notice on the landlord.

**Tues 4th July 2023** - Section 21 notice pushed through the door this morning. Says I have to be out by 4 September 2023. Two weeks after the council notice. Feels like revenge. Rang Shelter helpline.

**Sept 2023** - Council (Leanne) emailed back: the section 21 is invalid because it was served within six months of the improvement notice (retaliatory eviction - Deregulation Act 2015 s.33). So we can stay for now. Told Tom.

**Mon 2 Oct 2023** - Proper works finally started. DryRite again plus a window company. New extractor fans going in.
